# Notes

Research notes for this project, kept as a small wiki. Open this folder as an Obsidian vault ("Open folder as vault") or just read the Markdown.

## Where to start

- `Reading list.md` — ordered reading with pointers to paper pages. Add to it as you go.
- `Validation sets.md` — candidate ground-truth lists of landmark works.
- `Paper/` — one page per paper, named by citekey (`AuthorYearKeyword.md`).
- Topic pages at the root (`Disruption index.md`, `Word2vec.md`, ...).

## Conventions

- Frontmatter: `title`, `date`, `updated`. Bump `updated` on meaningful edits.
- Wikilinks use the full path from this folder: `[[Paper/Kim2026uncovering]]`, `[[Disruption index]]`.
- Body starts at H2; the frontmatter title is the H1.
- Paper pages: link line, author line, concept wikilinks, then a short summary in your own words.
- Math in `$...$` / `$$...$$`.
- Meeting or daily notes: `YYYY-MM-DD.md` at the root.
