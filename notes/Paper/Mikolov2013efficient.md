- Tomas Mikolov, Kai Chen, Greg Corrado, Jeffrey Dean. arXiv:1301.3781 (2013; v3 September 2013).
- https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.3781

[[Word2vec]]

The first word2vec paper. Proposes two architectures for learning continuous word vectors from very large corpora, CBOW and skip-gram, evaluated on word-similarity and analogy tasks. The headline result is training high-quality vectors on a 1.6-billion-word corpus in under a day, with state-of-the-art syntactic and semantic similarity. Read for the architectures; the training tricks are in [[Paper/Mikolov2013distributed]].
