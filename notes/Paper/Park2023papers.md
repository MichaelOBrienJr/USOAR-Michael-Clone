[[Disruption index]]

The most prominent application of the CD index: a regression-adjusted $CD_5$ trend over 1945–2011 that declines, read as science and technology becoming less disruptive. The claim is contested on measurement grounds (citation inflation, database artifacts), which is part of the motivation for a more robust measure in [[Paper/Kim2026uncovering]].
